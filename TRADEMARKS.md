# Names and logos

PlainSpice's code is MIT-licensed and its documentation is CC BY 4.0, but the names
**"PlainSpice"** and **"Solved Circuits"** and their logos are not licensed under either.

You may say that your project is "based on PlainSpice" or "uses circuits from Solved Circuits".
Please don't call a modified version "PlainSpice" or "Solved Circuits", use their logos, or
suggest that Solved Circuits made, checked or endorses it. The official PlainSpice is the one
published from this repository; the Solved Circuits videos' "Open this circuit" links always
point there.
