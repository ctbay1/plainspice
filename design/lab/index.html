Schematic lab

Every example netlist (examples/*/*.cir) and fixture (tests/fixtures/*.cir) drawn by the automatic layout of src/schematic/, in a neutral style: thin black lines, standard US/ANSI symbols, node names in italics, open circles for open ends, an open arrowhead where a node goes on to parts the drawing does not show (their names under the node name), and a small hop where two wires cross without joining. Under each drawing: what the check says (overlaps, and whether the drawing connects exactly what the netlist connects), the dot lines, and any part the drawing does not show. The last cards ("more") show shapes beyond the channel's circuits: a bridge, an amplifier, a star, a part typed into a drawn circuit, a drawing that gives one position, parts not drawn, an ammeter in a branch, and sources with AC values and transient functions.

Add ?boxes to the address to see the room kept for labels and results.
